# Decision examples

These are invented examples, not evidence about any actual study. Suggested rewrites depend on the stated context; they are not keyword replacement rules.

## Remove a writing decision, retain a settled definition

Source: “After discussing several names with the author, we decided to formally call this phenomenon cross-pair failure.”

If the supplied context confirms the term is final: “We term this phenomenon cross-pair failure.”

中文：「與作者討論後，我們決定正式將此現象命名為跨配對失敗。」→「本文將此現象稱為跨配對失敗。」

If the term is still tentative, do not assert it is final. Flag the unresolved name outside the manuscript.

## Replace an irrelevant denial only when the aim is known

Source: “We do not aim to outperform all segmentation methods. We evaluate generalization to unseen defect combinations.”

Revision: “We evaluate generalization to unseen defect combinations.”

中文：「本研究並非要超越所有分割方法，而是評估模型對未見缺陷組合的泛化能力。」→「本研究評估模型對未見缺陷組合的泛化能力。」

If the supplied text only says “We do not aim to outperform all methods,” there is insufficient information to invent an alternative objective. Flag the missing objective or remove the sentence only if surrounding text makes it redundant.

## Keep a limitation inside a defensive sentence

Source: “Reviewers should not criticize the lack of external validation because we only evaluated one hospital.”

Revision: “Evaluation was limited to one hospital; external validity remains untested.”

Do not reduce this to “We evaluated one hospital” if that would hide the inference boundary.

## Preserve methodological rationale

Keep: “We use a robust estimator because the residuals contain outliers.”

Keep: “We do not normalize each test sample independently because this would remove the amplitude differences under study.”

These communicate methodological choices, not conversations about manuscript wording.

## Preserve evidence and uncertainty

Keep: “The difference was not statistically significant (p = 0.18).”

Do not rewrite as “The methods perform equally well.”

Keep: “No external validation was performed.”

Do not replace with a positive-sounding sentence that implies external validation exists.

Keep: “These observational data do not establish causality.”

中文應保留：「此觀察性資料無法確立因果關係。」與「尚未評估模型在其他醫院的表現。」

## Remove author-directed commentary

Source: “As you suggested, this paragraph now emphasizes the generalization result. Accuracy on unseen combinations was 71%.”

Revision: “Accuracy on unseen combinations was 71%.”

Preserve the experimental conditions elsewhere in the source. Do not invent a baseline or claim improvement.

## Leave functional metadiscourse alone

Keep: “For clarity, let N denote the number of samples.”

Keep: “Section 4 presents the ablation results.”

Keep: “We call this failure mode X.”

These can help readers understand notation, structure, and definitions. Their appropriateness depends on the requested style, not a categorical ban.

## Respect genre and quoted material

A reviewer response may legitimately say “We have revised Section 3 to clarify the definition.” Do not strip revision history from that genre unless requested.

A direct quotation containing “we decided” should retain its quoted wording and attribution; assess the surrounding author prose instead.
